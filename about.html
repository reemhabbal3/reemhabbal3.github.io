RH · Bio

The person
behind the work.

I build the creative engine brands didn't know they needed, and the strategy behind why it works.

As a Forward Deployed Creative Lead at Unit Technologies, I sit at the intersection of strategy, production, and technology. I diagnose where a brand's creative is breaking down, then design the systems, from AI-powered workflows and autonomous agents to the underlying content strategy, that fix it and scale it. I work directly with clients to shape the thinking, then partner with our dev team to build the tools that bring it to life.

My path here wasn't linear, and that's the point. I've run high-volume retail teams, managed organic social for brands as a freelancer, and worked agency-side in paid media with each stop sharpening a different instinct: how to read an audience, how to build a system out of chaos, and how to turn a strategic idea into something that actually ships. A marketing and international business degree from FIU gave me the framework; the jobs gave me the reflexes.

I think globally, and I'm drawn to work that lives at the crossroads of culture and experience, from brand storytelling to the kind of large-scale events that turn a moment into a memory. I move quickly, I iterate in public, and I care as much about the "why" behind a creative decision as the execution. If you're looking for someone who can think strategically and still get their hands dirty in the build, that's me.

RH · Travel

Places that
shaped me.

Raised between cultures, I travel the same way I work - looking for what makes a place feel like itself. These are some of the moments that stuck.

Damascus, Syria

My birth city and one of the oldest cities in the world has shaped the way I perceive life, culture, and relationships.

Seoul, South Korea

Where modern meets culture, tradition and ever evolving trends. Seoul leads my fashion, skincare and balance of friendships.

Chengdu, China

The combination of art, fashion and history existing harmoniously has re-sparked my creativity and allowed me to try new things.

Alvor, Portugal

The first time I experienced the world from above, literally. I fulfilled my lifelong bucket list item and learned to be adventurous and try new things.
